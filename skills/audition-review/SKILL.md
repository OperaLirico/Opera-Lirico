# Audition and competition review

Verify the exact edition on the opera house, competition, academy, or organizer's official website. Capture role/voice type, age limits, repertoire, language, application materials, fee, audition format, audition location/date, application deadline, and official application route.

Keep auditions, competitions, chorus recruitment, young artist programs, and training courses correctly classified. Do not infer that an open call is suitable for every voice type. Preserve the original regulations PDF and record the page or section supporting eligibility and repertoire.

If the organizer page is inaccessible, the edition is unclear, or dates conflict, leave the record pending. Never turn an expired opportunity into an active one by changing dates. Clearly state whether the deadline is local time and use Europe/Rome only when applicable.
