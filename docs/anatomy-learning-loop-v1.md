# Anatomy learning-loop v1

Date: 2026-11-20  
Scope: `larynx-mechanism.html`

## What changed

- Added a searchable Chinese/English/abbreviation glossary for ten core laryngeal structures and muscle groups.
- Added a three-question client-side knowledge check covering PCA abduction, interarytenoid posterior closure, and CT-related vocal-fold length/tension.
- Preserved the existing institutional 3D models, five mechanism descriptions, evidence links, and provenance notes.
- Kept all quiz behavior local to the browser. No personal answers or scores are transmitted or stored.
- The controls explain anatomical concepts; they do not assess singing technique or individual health.

## Accuracy and limitations

- PCA is the principal vocal-fold abductor.
- Interarytenoid muscles contribute to approximation of the arytenoid cartilages and closure of the posterior intercartilaginous glottis.
- Cricothyroid contraction generally increases vocal-fold length and longitudinal tension; pitch is not controlled by CT alone.
- The glossary is a learning aid and should be read alongside the cited anatomy sources.
- No fabricated joint animation, force values, tissue deformation, or vocal-fold vibration simulation is introduced.
