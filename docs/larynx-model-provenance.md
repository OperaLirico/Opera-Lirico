# Laryngeal mechanism lab — asset provenance

## Primary 3D asset

- **Asset:** Larynx with Muscles and Ligaments
- **Creator / publisher:** University of Dundee School of Medicine, TILT / Centre for Anatomy and Human Identification (CAHID)
- **Medical artist:** Eve Laws
- **Original model:** https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/larynx-with-muscles-and-ligaments-3b247ff11b104e24acbb1c453f5bad46
- **Institutional project context:** https://www.dundee.ac.uk/tilt/medical-illustration/parapharyngeal-space
- **Model purpose:** ENT teaching; model covers laryngeal muscles, cartilages and ligaments.
- **Provenance note:** University of Dundee identifies this as one of its TILT/CAHID teaching models. The model is embedded from Sketchfab; OperaLab. does not download, modify, or redistribute the mesh.
- **Licence note:** A public legal-credit page identifies this Dundee model as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: https://masterclass.morgavi.fr/mentions-legales. The original Sketchfab model page remains the primary place to verify current model attribution and licence before any reuse beyond the embedded viewer. Non-commercial and ShareAlike conditions must be respected; re-check before any commercial use or asset extraction.
- **Presentation:** Third-party Sketchfab viewer embed; no local copy of the model.
- **Known limitation:** The model is a static anatomical reference. The mechanism controls on the page update explanatory text only; they do not animate the model or simulate validated cartilage motion, tissue deformation, vocal-fold vibration, or muscle force.

## Editorial rule

Do not describe this asset as a calibrated life-size specimen or a patient-specific reconstruction. Do not infer exact dimensions from the viewer. If future interactive movement is added, use independently validated joint axes, attachment data, motion ranges, and a review by a qualified anatomist or laryngologist. Keep static anatomical truth visually distinct from explanatory motion graphics.
